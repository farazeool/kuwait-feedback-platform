1. Introduction

What is this system?

Imagine you own a restaurant in Kuwait. Every day, customers come in, eat your food, and leave. Some are happy. Some are not. But mostly, they just leave without telling you what they thought.

This system is like a digital suggestion box that never gets lost and never gets ignored. It automatically organizes all customer feedback so you can actually use it to make your business better.

🤔 Think of it this way: It's a suggestion box + automatic report card + alarm system all in one.

2. System Overview

The complete workflow

Let's walk through how the entire system works, from start to finish:

  1. Set up: An admin creates the organization in the system, adds locations (branches), and creates a survey with questions.
  2. Publish: The admin publishes the survey and generates a QR code. The QR code is printed and placed on tables or at the counter.
  3. Customer scans: A customer scans the QR code with their phone — no app needed, just the camera. The survey opens in their browser.
  4. Customer submits: The customer answers the questions (tap a rating, select choices, type a comment) and clicks Submit.
  5. System records: The system stores the response with the date, time, location, and channel. It also checks if the rating is low enough to trigger an alert.
  6. Manager sees it: The response appears on the manager's dashboard immediately. If it triggered an alert, the manager gets notified.
  7. Admin reviews: The organization admin can compare all locations, view charts and trends, and make data-driven decisions.
🔄 How Data Flows:
Customer Phone → Public Survey Page → System Database → Dashboard → Analytics → Manager

4. Getting Started

Logging in for the first time

  1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, or Edge)
  2. Go to the web address your administrator gave you
  3. You will see the Login page with fields for email and password
  4. Enter your email address and password
  5. Click Sign In
💡 Don't have an account yet? Your organization admin must invite you. They will send you an email with a special invitation link. Click the link to accept and create your account.

Your first look at the Dashboard

After logging in, you land on the Dashboard. This is your home page. Here's what you'll see:

📊 Dashboard Overview:
📈 Response Trend Chart • 📊 Rating Distribution • 📍 Location Comparison
🏆 KPI Cards • 📝 Recent Responses Table • ⚠️ Alert Count

Creating your first survey

  1. Click Surveys in the menu on the left
  2. Click the New Survey button
  3. Choose a template (Cafe/Restaurant, Retail, etc.) or start from scratch
  4. Give your survey a name in English (and optionally Arabic)
  5. Select which location this survey is for
  6. Add questions: Rating (e.g., 1-5 stars), Multiple Choice, or Text
  7. Click Save Draft to save, or Publish to make it live

Sharing the survey with customers

  1. Open your published survey
  2. Click Distribution or View QR Code
  3. Download the QR code as an image (SVG or PNG)
  4. Print it and place it at your location — on tables, at the counter, near the exit
  5. When customers scan the QR code with their phone, they can submit feedback instantly
✅ You're now collecting feedback! Responses will appear on your Dashboard automatically.

5. Feature Documentation

Here is every feature in the system, explained simply.

📊 Dashboard

What it is: Your home page — the first thing you see when you log in.

Why it exists: Instead of clicking through many pages to find information, the dashboard brings everything important to one screen.

What you see: KPI cards (total responses, average rating, open alerts), charts (response trends, rating distribution, location comparison), and a table of recent responses.

📋 Surveys

What it is: A survey is a set of questions you create to collect feedback from customers.

Types of questions you can add:

  • Rating: Customer picks a number (e.g., 1-5 stars)
  • Multiple Choice: Customer picks from a list of options
  • Text: Customer types their own answer

Statuses: Draft (still working), Active (live and collecting), Archived (closed, but data kept)

📨 Responses

What it is: One customer's completed survey with all their answers.

Why it exists: Scattered feedback becomes organized, searchable records you can filter and act on.

Workflow statuses: Monitor Only, Branch Follow-up, Controlled Investigation, Immediate Escalation

🚨 Alerts

What it is: An automatic warning created when a customer gives a very low rating.

Why it exists: You need to know about problems immediately — not a week later when you check reports.

Statuses: Open (new), Acknowledged (being handled), Resolved (fixed), Dismissed (not an issue)

📈 Analytics

What it is: Charts and graphs that show how your business is doing over time.

What you can see: Response trends, rating distribution, location comparison, survey comparison, low-score trends, alert metrics.

Filters: You can filter by date range, location, survey, rating range, and alert status.

📌 Normalized Ratings: Different surveys might use 1-5, 1-10, or other scales. The system converts everything to a 0-100% scale so you can compare them fairly. A 4/5 = 80%.

🏆 KPI Dashboard

What it is: Key Performance Indicators — the most important numbers for your business.

What you see: Total responses, average rating, satisfaction percentage, negative feedback percentage, top concerns, location KPIs, channel breakdown.

⚡ Quick Feedback (One-Tap)

What it is: A super-simple way for customers to give feedback with just one tap — no forms, no typing. Just tap a smiley face.

Why it exists: Long surveys can be annoying for busy customers. Quick feedback increases response rates.

How it works: Customer taps 😊 (good), 😐 (okay), or 😡 (bad) → optionally adds a comment → done in 2 seconds.

🔗 Distribution

What it is: A system for creating trackable feedback links for each employee, location, or touchpoint.

Why it exists: If Employee A gets 50 responses and Employee B gets 5, Employee A is doing a better job engaging customers.

What you can track: Clicks, responses, conversion rate, employee attribution, location attribution.

📍 Touchpoints

What it is: A specific place where customers interact with your business — like the front counter, a service desk, or a drive-through.

Why it exists: If you have different departments, you need to know which one is doing well and which needs improvement.

🏢 Departments

What it is: A sub-group within a location — for example, a Kitchen department and a Service department within a restaurant.

How it differs from a Touchpoint: A touchpoint is where customers give feedback. A department is who the feedback is about.

📢 Campaigns

What it is: A targeted feedback collection effort — for example, sending an email to recent customers asking them to fill out a survey.

Why it exists: Instead of waiting for customers to find your QR code, campaigns let you actively reach out to them.

📥 Export

What it is: Download your data as a CSV file that opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

When to use it: When you need to create custom reports, share data with a consultant, or archive data.

⚠️ Note: The export respects your current filters. If you filtered by a specific location, only that location's data is exported.

👥 Team

What it is: Manage who has access to your organization in the system.

How to invite someone: Go to Team → Invite Member → Enter email → Choose role → Send. They receive an email with an invitation link.

Important: You cannot remove the last owner of an organization.

⚙️ Settings

What it is: Where you configure your organization, locations, branding, and preferences.

What you can change: Organization name (English/Arabic), logo, brand colors, location details, governorate, area, thank-you messages, and more.

🎨 Branding

What it is: Customizing how the feedback page looks to your customers — with your logo, colors, and messages.

Why it exists: When a customer scans your QR code, the survey should look like YOUR business, not a generic form.

📱 QR Code

What it is: The system generates QR code images that link to your surveys.

How to use: Download as SVG (best for printing) or PNG, then print and display at your location.

🖥️ Kiosk Mode

What it is: A full-screen feedback page designed for tablets or touchscreens mounted at your business.

How it works: A tablet sits at the exit with the kiosk page open. Customers tap their rating on the screen as they leave. The screen auto-resets for the next person.

⚡ Escalation Rules

What it is: Automatic triggers that take action when certain conditions are met — like a very negative response that mentions specific keywords.

Example: IF rating is 1/5 AND comment contains "food poisoning" THEN create an alert AND notify the manager immediately.

🔧 Corrective Actions

What it is: A formal task to fix a problem identified through customer feedback.

Lifecycle: Open → In Progress → Under Review → Closed

When to use: When a problem needs more than a quick fix — it needs investigation, a plan, and verification.

🔍 Investigations

What it is: A formal inquiry into a problem. More detailed than a corrective action because you're still figuring out what happened.

📎 Evidence

What it is: Proof that something happened — a photo, PDF, training record, maintenance log, or other file.

Why it exists: When you say "we fixed the broken air conditioner," you attach a photo to prove it.

✅ Pilot Checklist

What it is: A to-do list that appears on the dashboard for new organizations.

The four steps: 1. Set up a location → 2. Create a survey → 3. Publish the survey → 4. Collect a response. The checklist disappears when all steps are done.

🚀 Onboarding

What it is: The first-time setup process when a new organization joins the platform.

What you fill in: Organization name, slug (web address), business category, phone number, first location name and address. Everything is created at once.

8. Hardware Integration

What hardware does this system work with?

💻 Computers and Laptops

Used by: Admins, managers, and staff to manage the system.

Requirements: Any computer with a web browser and internet connection.

If it fails: Try a different browser. Clear your cache. Check your internet.

📱 Smartphones (Customers)

Used by: Customers to scan QR codes and submit feedback.

How it works: Customer opens camera → scans QR code → phone opens survey in browser. No app needed!

If it fails: Make sure the QR code is clean and well-lit. Customer can also type the URL directly.

📟 Tablets (Kiosk Mode)

Used by: Customers at a dedicated feedback station in your business.

Setup: Mount a tablet near the exit. Open the kiosk URL. The page shows large, easy-to-tap buttons. After each response, it auto-resets.

🖨️ Printed QR Code Cards

Used by: Customers scan with their phone.

Best practices: Print on card stock, laminate for durability, place at eye level, ensure good lighting. Include simple instructions in English and Arabic near the QR code.

📶 Network Equipment

What it does: Provides internet for the system to work.

If it fails: Responses cannot be submitted until internet is restored — but no data is lost. Staff can write feedback manually and enter it later.

9. Daily Operations Guide

A Day in the Life

🌅 Morning (8:00 AM - 10:00 AM)

Log in and check the Dashboard. Look for new responses overnight. Check for open alerts. Read customer comments. Note any recurring themes.

☀️ Midday (10:00 AM - 2:00 PM)

Ensure QR codes are visible on tables. Check that the kiosk tablet is working. Monitor the dashboard as responses come in.

🌤️ Afternoon (2:00 PM - 5:00 PM)

Lunch rush brings more responses. If a low rating triggers an alert, respond right away if needed.

🌙 Evening (5:00 PM - 7:00 PM)

Review the day's data: total responses, average rating, new alerts, comments. Plan improvements based on feedback patterns.

Weekly Tasks

  • Review the KPI Dashboard (satisfaction %, response volume, location comparison)
  • Check team assignments — any new staff to invite?
  • Compare this week's analytics to last week's

Monthly Tasks

  • Generate monthly analytics and review all locations
  • Review surveys — are existing ones still relevant? Need new ones?
  • Audit team access — remove inactive users
  • Update branding if needed

  2. Click Invite Member
  3. Enter their email address and choose their role
  4. Click Send — They receive an email invitation
  5. They click the link, set their password, and they're in

Adding a New Location

  1. Go to Settings → Locations

  3. Enter the name (English and Arabic), governorate, area, and address
  4. Click Save

Archiving a Survey

  1. Go to Surveys
  2. Find the survey and click Archive
  3. All responses are kept — the survey just stops accepting new ones

The system runs on Supabase, which automatically creates daily backups. For paid plans, you can restore data from any point in the last 7-30 days.

What you should also do: Export data regularly via CSV. Keep notes of important settings.

11. Troubleshooting

Cannot log in

Possible causes: Wrong email, wrong password, account not activated, account was removed, system is down.

Solutions: Check your email and password. Try "Forgot password." Contact your admin. Wait and retry.

Dashboard is empty

Possible causes: No responses yet, date filter set wrong, you don't have permission, or a filter is active.

Solutions: Check the date filter. Reset all filters. Check your role (manager sees only their location).

Survey not showing to customers

Possible causes: Survey is in Draft, Archived, or the QR code is damaged.

Solutions: Check survey status (must be "Active"). Get the correct link. Test the QR code with your own phone. Reprint if damaged.

Customer says survey won't load

Possible causes: Slow internet, outdated browser, or a temporary system issue.

Solutions: Check the survey on your own phone. Provide the direct URL (instead of QR code). Simplify the survey if it has too many questions.

False alert

Possible causes: Customer accidentally submitted low rating, or threshold is set too high.

Solutions: Read the response to check. Dismiss the alert if not relevant. Adjust the threshold in Settings.

Email invitation not received

Possible causes: Wrong email address, went to spam, or SMTP provider not configured.

Solutions: Check the email address. Check spam folder. Click Resend in Team > Invitations.

System is slow

Solutions: Refresh the page. Check your internet. Try a different browser. Narrow your filters (smaller date range loads faster).

12. Security

How your data stays safe

Security FeatureWhat it does
User AccountsEach person has their own account. You know who did what, and you can remove access when someone leaves.
PasswordsMust be at least 12 characters. Stored encrypted (not saved as plain text). Use a different password than other services.
Role-Based AccessManagers see only their location. Admins see everything. People can only see what they need.
Tenant IsolationDifferent organizations cannot see each other's data. Enforced at the database level.
EncryptionData is encrypted between your browser and the system (HTTPS). Data at rest is encrypted in the database.
Audit LoggingEvery important action is recorded — who logged in, who changed settings, who exported data.

What the system does NOT store

  • ❌ Customer names (feedback is anonymous)
  • ❌ Customer personal information
  • ❌ Payment or credit card information
  • ❌ Plain-text passwords

Security Best Practices

  • ✅ Use strong passwords (12+ characters, mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
  • ✅ Don't share your password — each person should have their own account
  • ✅ Log out when you leave your computer
  • ✅ Report suspicious activity to your admin
  • ✅ Use a password manager
